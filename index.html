Ethical Hooper Index

Score = 80% field-goal share of points + 20% foul-drawing rate, then converted to a percentile against the players currently shown below, so narrowing the filters to a more competitive group raises everyone's score accordingly (it's relative to who's on screen, not a fixed number). "Foul rate %ile" is the plain percentile of a player's FTA/FGA rate within the qualified pool: higher values mean they draw fouls more often per shot than more of the field. Color runs on that same percentile gradient. "Avg" combines all six seasons per player, weighted by games played in each season (not a simple average across years).

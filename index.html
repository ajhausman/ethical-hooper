Ethical Hooper Index

Score = 80% field-goal share of points + 20% foul-drawing rate, then stretched 0-100 against the league's go-to scorers (top 10% by PPG) so the most and least foul-reliant scorers spread across the full scale. "Foul rate %ile" is the plain percentile of a player's FTA/FGA rate within the qualified pool -- higher means they draw fouls more often per shot than more of the field. Color runs on a continuous percentile gradient, not fixed bands. "Avg" combines all six seasons per player, weighted by games played in each season (not a simple average across years).
